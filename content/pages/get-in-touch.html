    FAQs

    What services does MM Digital offer?

    We provide a full suite of digital marketing and creative services designed to deliver structured, measurable growth.
     
    Our services include:
    Website design & development
    Branding & brand strategy
    SEO
    Social media management
    Content creation
    PPC advertising
    Email marketing
    Full marketing retainers
     
    We build end-to-end solutions, not isolated tactics, ensuring every channel works together to support your commercial objectives.

    What industries do you work with?

    We work across a wide range of industries, including retail, hospitality, property, tech, manufacturing, professional services and more.
     
    Our approach is adaptable and strategy-led, meaning we tailor campaigns to your specific audience, market position and growth targets.
     
    However, we do not work with businesses in the gambling, adult entertainment or industries that conflict with our ethical standards and values.

    How do you approach a new project?

    Every project begins with clarity.
     
    We take time to understand:
    Your goals
    Your audience
    Your current performance
    Your positioning within the market
     
    From there, we build a tailored strategy and roadmap. You remain involved at every key stage, from concept and design through to launch, optimisation and performance tracking.
     
    Our focus is not just on delivery, it’s on long-term impact.

    Do you offer one-off projects or ongoing retainers?

    Both.
     
    We deliver standalone projects such as branding and website builds.
     
    However, the strongest and most consistent results come from ongoing retainers, where strategy, execution and optimisation work together long-term.
     
    Marketing is not a short-term exercise, it is a structured growth system.

    Do you offer ongoing support after a project is complete?

    Yes.
     
    We offer ongoing website maintenance, marketing support and strategic retainers to ensure performance remains strong over time.
     
    Retainer clients benefit from continuous optimisation, proactive adjustments and uninterrupted campaign management, ensuring their digital presence remains competitive and effective.

    Can you redesign my existing website?

    Absolutely.
     
    We can refresh your existing website to improve design, user experience, conversion performance and technical structure, while maintaining or refining your existing brand identity.
     
    Our goal is not just to modernise appearance, but to improve performance.

    Do you work with small businesses as well as large companies?

    Yes.
     
    We work with ambitious businesses of all sizes, from start-ups building strong foundations to established brands looking to scale.
     
    What matters most is not company size, but clarity of intent and commitment to growth.

    How much do your services cost?

    Pricing depends on scope, objectives and scale.
     
    We provide transparent proposals with no hidden fees. During our discovery process, we outline what level of investment is required to realistically achieve your goals.
     
    We focus on value and return, not simply cost.

    How quickly will we see results?

    Results depend on the strategy and channel mix.
     
    Paid advertising can generate traction quickly when structured properly.
    SEO, branding and authority-building are longer-term drivers.
     
    We set clear milestones and expectations from the outset, ensuring performance is measurable and aligned with commercial outcomes.

    What makes MM Digital different from other agencies?

    We combine creativity with data-driven strategy.
     
    Every campaign is built around:
     
    Clear commercial objectives
    Measurable performance tracking
    Intelligent use of AI
    Structured growth systems
    Strong brand positioning
     
    We are known for our personal approach, clear communication and commitment to quality, but ultimately, what sets us apart is structure and accountability.
