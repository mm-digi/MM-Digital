Client Background

Vaala had real potential and a clear ambition. What it needed was marketing that delivered more than visibility: marketing that delivered genuine results.

Vaala came to us looking to build its presence and, more importantly, to turn that presence into real business: more traffic, more enquiries, more growth. The ambition was there, and what it needed was a focused, results-driven approach to make it happen.

Our brief was to grow Vaala’s reach, drive quality traffic to the website, and convert that attention into a steady stream of enquiries.

The Challenge

Reach on its own is easy to chase and easy to waste. Vaala needed marketing that did more than rack up views. It needed to bring the right people to the website and turn them into genuine enquiries. That meant being targeted, consistent, and focused on the metrics that actually matter to the business.

The challenge was to grow social reach meaningfully, convert that into real website traffic, and make sure it translated into enquiries, building momentum that compounds rather than a short-lived spike.

Our Approach

  • Social media management and content
  • Paid advertising
  • Lead generation
  • Website traffic growth
  • Ongoing optimisation

We approached Vaala with a clear, results-first focus: reach the right people, bring them to the website, and turn them into enquiries.

On social, we built a consistent, engaging presence and grew Vaala’s reach to over 1 million total social media views, putting the brand in front of a far wider, well-targeted audience. Rather than chasing vanity reach, we focused that growth on driving genuine action.

The results followed. We delivered a 12% increase in website traffic and an 8% increase in enquiries, clear proof that the reach we built was translating into real business. We continue to refine the approach, sharpening the targeting and building on that momentum.

Today, Vaala is growing with purpose: more reach, more traffic and more enquiries. And we’re not done yet.
