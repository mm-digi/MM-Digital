Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how MM Digital Marketing Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, www.mm-digi.co.uk. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal data more generally.

By using our website, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy, except for any non essential cookies, which we only set with your consent.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to improve performance, and to provide information to the site owner.

We also use similar technologies such as tracking pixels and tags, which work in a similar way. In this policy, we refer to all of these simply as “cookies”.

Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Make our website function properly and securely
  • Understand how visitors use our website, so we can improve it
  • Measure the performance of our content and marketing
  • Support our advertising and remarketing activity

The Types of Cookies We Use

Essential cookies

These are necessary for our website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as navigating the site or filling in forms. Without these, parts of the website will not work. These do not require your consent.

Analytics cookies

These help us understand how visitors interact with our website, such as which pages are visited and how people move around the site. This helps us improve how the website works. These are only set with your consent.

Marketing cookies

These are used to deliver and measure advertising that is relevant to you, and to understand the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns, including remarketing. These are only set with your consent.

Third Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are set by trusted third parties who provide services to us. These currently include:

  • Google Analytics, to measure and analyse website usage and performance
  • Meta Pixel, to measure and support advertising and remarketing across Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram)

These third parties process data in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies. We recommend reviewing their policies for further detail on how they use data.

Your Consent

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie banner allowing you to accept or manage your cookie preferences. Essential cookies will always be active, as they are required for the site to function. Non essential cookies (analytics and marketing) will only be set once you have given your consent.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your cookie preferences or your browser settings.

Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Through our cookie banner / preferences, where available on our website
  • Through your browser settings, where you can block or delete cookies. Most browsers allow you to refuse or remove cookies, though doing so may affect how the website functions. Instructions can usually be found in your browser’s help section.

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for operational, legal or regulatory reasons. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.
