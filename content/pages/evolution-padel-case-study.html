Client Background

Before a new venture can open its doors, everything has to be built from the ground up. For Evolution Padel, full of ambition and potential, all of that was still ahead.

Evolution Padel arrived ahead of launch with a clear vision and real drive, and a need for the complete foundations of a brand: the identity, the branding, and the presence to build anticipation before launch. There was no established brand yet, just the opportunity to create one properly, from scratch.

Our brief was to develop the full brand and branding, and to build genuine visibility and momentum in the crucial pre-launch period, so that Evolution Padel would open to a warm, engaged audience rather than a standing start.

The Challenge

Launching a new brand is one of the hardest things to get right. Everything has to be built at once – the identity, the look, the voice – and it all has to be in place before a single customer walks through the door. Get the pre-launch wrong, and you open to silence. Get it right, and you open to an audience already excited to be there.

The challenge was to create the entire brand from scratch and, at the same time, build real visibility and anticipation ahead of launch, so that Evolution Padel arrived with momentum, not from zero.

Our Approach

  • Full brand setup and identity
  • Complete branding from scratch
  • Social media and content
  • Pre-launch marketing
  • Visibility and anticipation building

We treated Evolution Padel as a complete brand build paired with a focused pre-launch strategy.

We started with the brand itself, creating the full identity and branding from the ground up: the look, the feel and the personality that would define Evolution Padel. Everything was built to be distinctive, consistent and ready to carry the brand from launch and beyond.

With the brand in place, we turned to building anticipation. Through consistent content and a focused pre-launch presence across social, we grew the brand’s visibility ahead of opening, delivering a 32% increase in pre-launch visibility. Rather than opening to an empty room, Evolution Padel built a genuine, engaged audience before day one: exactly the foundation a new venture needs.

Evolution Padel opened as a fully formed brand with real momentum behind it, and we’re helping it build on that start.
