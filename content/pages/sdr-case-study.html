Client Background

SDR, The Car Buying Service, knows exactly what it does well, and it wanted a digital setup designed not just to be seen, but to generate real leads and sales.

SDR is a car buying service built on trust and a straightforward promise to its customers. What it needed was a digital engine to match: a setup that could consistently attract the right people and turn that attention into genuine, qualified leads.

Our brief was not simply to raise awareness. It was to build the machine behind the growth: a full custom sales channel designed to drive leads and conversions, and to keep improving it over time.

The Challenge

For a service business, visibility alone means nothing without conversion. SDR needed every bit of attention to work hard, to attract the right customers and move them smoothly from interest to enquiry to sale. Generic marketing would not cut it. This required a purpose-built sales channel designed around how SDR actually wins business.

The challenge was to build that channel from scratch, generate a steady flow of quality leads, and continually sharpen conversions, so that growth was not just about reach, but about real, trackable results.

Our Approach

  • Full custom sales channel build
  • Paid advertising
  • Social media and content
  • SEO and local findability
  • Lead generation and conversion optimisation

We treated SDR as a conversion-first partnership, building the sales engine and then optimising it relentlessly.

At the core, we developed a full custom sales channel, built specifically around how SDR attracts and converts customers. Rather than a generic funnel, this was a purpose-built system designed to capture interest and turn it into genuine enquiries.

Around that, we drove quality traffic through paid advertising, social media and SEO, and focused hard on conversion. The results tell the story: over 5,000 leads generated, and a 4% increase in conversions, meaning more of that traffic turned into real business. This was not about vanity reach. It was about building a channel that genuinely performs.

SDR now has a custom-built sales engine that consistently delivers leads and conversions, and we keep refining it to deliver more.
