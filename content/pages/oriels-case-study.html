Client Background

Oriels opened in 2025 as a proudly independent cocktail bar and venue in the heart of Exeter. The idea was a room with a point of view: crafted cocktails, live music, stand-up comedy and food, open from daytime brunch through to the late night.

It sits on the High Street, in a city that already has plenty of places to go. The venue needed to feel premium without feeling closed off, and familiar enough that Exeter would make it part of the week.

The Challenge

A new venue has to be known before anyone walks through the door. Oriels needed a name people recognised, a website that could take a booking, and a following in a busy nightlife city.

There was no brand in the world yet, no site, and no audience. The launch had to arrive with an identity, a lineup and a reason for Exeter to show up on night one, then keep showing up for brunch, comedy and the late shows.

Our Approach

  • Brand identity
  • Website and reservations
  • Facebook and Instagram
  • Live music, comedy and events
  • Launch and ongoing marketing

We built the brand and the digital setup together, before the doors opened. That meant the visual identity, the website and reservations, the social channels, and a launch plan shaped around the drinks, the music and the weekly events.

When Oriels opened in 2025, it already had a public face. From there the work turned to keeping the room full. Content, campaigns and the weekly lineup have carried the venue since, across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok, from bottomless brunch and comedy club to Friday night live and the late programme.

That work has put Oriels in front of the city. The channels have passed 6.8 million social views, and the website has taken more than 350,000 visits, turning attention into bookings and nights out.

A new venue, taken from a name to one of Exeter’s rooms people plan around. That is exactly what we do.
