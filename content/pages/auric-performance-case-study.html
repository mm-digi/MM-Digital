Client Background

Every brand starts somewhere. For Auric Performance, it started with real ambition, a clear vision and a name, and nothing else yet built. Starting from a blank page is one of our favourite briefs.

Auric Performance arrived with drive and a clear sense of where it wanted to go, and a need for everything that sits beneath a successful brand. There was no identity out in the world, no website, no digital presence, just a name and an ambition waiting to be brought to life.

Our brief was not to tweak or refine an existing brand. It was to create Auric Performance from scratch: the identity, the website and the digital presence, and to launch it as a complete, credible business ready to compete.

The Challenge

Building a brand from nothing means getting everything right at once. A new brand has to look established before anyone has heard of it, earn trust before it has a track record, and present itself with the confidence of a business that has been around for years, all from a standing start.

The challenge was to build the entire foundation: a strong, distinctive brand identity, a full website developed from the ground up, and a digital presence credible enough to launch with impact. Everything had to work together, so that the moment Auric Performance went live, it arrived as a finished, professional brand rather than a work in progress.

Our Approach

  • Full brand identity and launch
  • Complete website development
  • Social media and content
  • Digital presence setup
  • Ongoing support and growth

We treated Auric Performance as a complete brand build, creating everything from the ground up and launching it properly.

We started with the brand itself, developing a full identity from scratch: the look, the feel and the personality that would set Auric Performance apart. Around that, we built the complete website, developed from the ground up to present the brand professionally and give it a strong, credible home online.

With the foundations in place, we launched Auric Performance into the world as a finished, fully operational brand, and built its digital presence across social. The response speaks for itself: the website has driven over 500,000 visits, a level of traffic that reflects a brand that arrived with real impact and continues to attract genuine attention.

Auric Performance went from a name to a fully launched brand, built entirely from scratch and already proving its reach. We’re still by its side as it grows.
