Client Background

Yvonne Coomber is a Devon-based contemporary artist. Her paintings are full of wildflowers, colour and light, and they are collected as originals and prints.

She shows the work from her gallery in Totnes, and through exhibitions, and sells it from her website. The online presence had to feel as considered as the paintings.

The Challenge

Interest in the paintings was already there. What the brand needed was a steadier digital presence, so new collectors could find the work and the people who already loved it stayed close.

The channels had to keep Yvonne’s voice, and the website had to turn that attention into visits to the shop for originals, prints and canvases.

Our Approach

  • Social media strategy
  • Content and storytelling
  • Audience growth
  • Website and print shop

We put a clear plan behind the channels. Content follows the paintings, the studio and the stories around them, across Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, LinkedIn, YouTube and X.

The website brings those people on to the shop. Originals, prints and canvases sit at the end of the same path as the social posts, so looking at the work can become a visit.

That work has widened the reach of the paintings. The channels have reached more than 4.9 million art lovers, and audience engagement is up by more than 57%.

A body of work, taken further into the world while keeping its heart. That is exactly what we do.
