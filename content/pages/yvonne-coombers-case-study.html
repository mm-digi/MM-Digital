Expanding Yvonne Coomber’s Digital Presence

Introduction

Yvonne Coomber is a renowned British contemporary artist whose colourful and uplifting paintings have gained international recognition. As demand for her artwork continued to grow, MM Digital partnered with Yvonne to strengthen her online presence and connect her work with a wider global audience through strategic digital marketing and social media growth.

Client Overview

Yvonne Coomber’s artwork is celebrated for its vibrant colour, emotional depth, and connection to nature. Her pieces are collected worldwide, with prints and original artworks sold through galleries, exhibitions, and her online store. As the brand expanded digitally, the objective was to ensure her online presence matched the quality and reach of her artistic reputation.

Challenges and Objectives

Before working with MM Digital, Yvonne’s digital presence needed further structure and consistency to support growing international interest. Key challenges included:

• Creating a cohesive digital strategy across platforms. • Increasing engagement with a highly visual audience. • Reaching new art collectors beyond existing gallery audiences. • Strengthening her social media presence while maintaining authenticity. • Supporting online traffic to her website and print store.

The objective was to expand her digital footprint while maintaining the integrity and emotion behind the brand.

MM Digital’s Approach

MM Digital developed a strategy focused on storytelling, audience engagement, and consistent digital growth.

Social Media Strategy: Developed a structured posting and engagement plan across social platforms to showcase artwork, studio insights, and creative inspiration.
Content Development: Created engaging visual content that highlighted Yvonne’s artistic process and storytelling behind each piece.
Audience Growth: Implemented audience growth strategies targeting art collectors and enthusiasts.
Website Traffic Support: Directed engaged social audiences towards her website and shop, supporting print and artwork sales.

Results and Impact

The strategy successfully expanded Yvonne’s reach and engagement online.

Key results include:

• 282,000+ art lovers reached across social media • 40%+ increase in audience engagement • Significant growth in online interaction and brand visibility • A stronger digital presence supporting exhibitions, launches, and artwork releases

Conclusion

Through a carefully structured digital strategy, MM Digital helped amplify Yvonne Coomber’s artistic presence online, connecting her work with a wider global audience while maintaining the authenticity and emotion at the heart of her brand.
