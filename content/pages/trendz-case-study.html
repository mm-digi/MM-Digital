Client Background

Trendz is a pre-launch product for people who watch crypto and social trends. It scores a viral clip or post, then turns a winning idea into a Solana token launch.

Before the product opened, it needed a public face. One site, one story, and a list of people ready for the first day.

The Challenge

A product that is not open yet still has to be understood. The audience is already on X and across social platforms, and it moves quickly.

The site had to explain the idea on one screen, and give people a reason to join the waitlist before launch day.

Our Approach

  • Waitlist website
  • Pre-launch story
  • Crypto and social
  • Early access list

We built the pre-launch site around one action: join the waitlist. The page shows the product, the platforms it watches, and the path from a viral signal to a launch.

Social carries the same story. The posts and the website point at the same early-access list, so the hype has somewhere to land.

The waitlist is live, and the launch story is in market. That is the work before the doors open.
