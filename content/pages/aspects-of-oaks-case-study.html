Client Background

Some brands need help finding something to say. Aspects of Oak already had it: real craftsmanship, a quality product and a loyal reputation. What it wanted was to reach more of the right people and turn that reputation into genuine enquiries.

The product and the reputation were strong, but there was real opportunity to extend the brand’s reach, put it in front of more of the right audience, and convert that visibility into consistent enquiries and leads.

Our brief was to grow Aspects of Oak’s digital presence, drive quality leads, and increase enquiries, turning genuine craftsmanship into genuine business growth.

The Challenge

For a craftsmanship-led brand, the quality speaks for itself, but only once the right people see it. Aspects of Oak needed to reach a wider, well-targeted audience, showcase its work in a way that did it justice, and crucially, convert that attention into real enquiries rather than just views.

The challenge was to grow reach meaningfully while keeping it targeted, and to make sure that growth translated into genuine business: more leads and more enquiries, not just bigger numbers.

Our Approach

  • Social media management and content
  • Paid advertising
  • Website support
  • Lead generation
  • Enquiry growth and optimisation

We approached Aspects of Oak with a clear focus: grow reach, but make it count.

On content and social, we built a consistent presence that showcased the brand’s craftsmanship and quality, reaching far more of the right people. Through strong content and targeted paid advertising, we grew the brand to over 1 million total views, putting Aspects of Oak firmly in front of its ideal audience.

Crucially, we focused on turning that reach into results. The work generated over 1,400 leads and drove a 16% increase in enquiries: proof that the growth was about real, measurable business impact, not just visibility.

Aspects of Oak now has the reach, the leads and the enquiries to match the quality of its work, and that growth is still building.
