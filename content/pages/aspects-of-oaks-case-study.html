Strengthening Aspects of Oak’s Online Presence for High-End Carpentry & Construction

Introduction

Aspects of Oak is a carpentry and construction company specialising in high-quality bespoke projects across the South West. MM Digital partnered with the business to strengthen its digital presence and increase project enquiries.

Client Overview

The company focuses on premium carpentry, renovations, and construction work, delivering craftsmanship-driven projects for residential clients. Building a stronger digital presence was key to reaching homeowners seeking high-end building services.

Challenges and Objectives

Key challenges included:

• Limited digital visibility within the local market • Generating consistent project enquiries • Showcasing high-quality craftsmanship through digital channels • Competing with other local construction businesses online

The objective was to increase visibility and attract high-value enquiries.

MM Digital’s Approach

MM Digital implemented a focused digital strategy.

Website Development: Built a professional website showcasing completed projects and services.
Social Media Strategy: Developed content highlighting craftsmanship and project transformations.
Local Marketing: Targeted homeowners within the South West region.
Brand Positioning: Strengthened the company’s positioning as a premium carpentry provider.

Conclusion

Through strategic digital marketing and brand positioning, MM Digital helped Aspects of Oak strengthen its online presence and attract new project opportunities.
