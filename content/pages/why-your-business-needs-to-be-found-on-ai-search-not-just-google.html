Why Your Business Needs to Be Found on AI Search (Not Just Google)

Published by MM Digital

September 2026

The rise of GEO and what it means for small businesses in 2026.

If you have been focused on getting your business to the top of Google, you are thinking in the right direction. But in 2026, Google is no longer the only place your potential customers are searching. For a growing number of people, it is not even the first place they look.

Welcome to the age of AI search.

What Is AI Search and Why Does It Matter?

AI search refers to platforms that answer questions using artificial intelligence, often providing a direct response supported by information from across the web. ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot are all changing the way people research businesses, compare options and make decisions.

Instead of working through a traditional page of links, a user can ask a detailed question and receive a clear summary in seconds. Those answers may include citations, supporting links, map results, products or business recommendations.

If your business is understood as a relevant, trustworthy source, it has a chance to appear in that journey. If AI platforms cannot confidently understand who you are, what you offer or where you operate, your competitors may be given the visibility instead.

This is not a future trend. It is happening now.

The Rise of GEO: Generative Engine Optimisation

Traditional SEO, or search engine optimisation, has been a cornerstone of digital marketing for more than two decades. Its goal is to help your website appear prominently when someone searches for the products or services you provide.

GEO, or Generative Engine Optimisation, is the practice of making your digital presence easier for AI-powered search experiences to discover, understand and reference when producing an answer.

Imagine someone asking for a digital marketing agency in Exeter, a padel club near Glasgow or a premium dog accessories brand in the UK. You want your business to be considered when the answer is created. GEO is the work that improves your chances of being found.

How Is GEO Different From Traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on areas such as technical performance, site structure, helpful content, links and relevance. These foundations still matter. In fact, Google has made it clear that established SEO best practice remains important for appearing in its generative AI features.

GEO widens the focus. It considers how clearly your whole online presence communicates what you do, who you serve, where you operate and why you are credible. That includes your website, business listings, reviews, third-party mentions, social profiles and the consistency of information across them all.

There is no magic GEO switch and nobody can guarantee inclusion in an AI-generated answer. The strongest approach is to build the kind of clear, useful and trustworthy digital footprint that both people and search systems can understand.

What Does This Mean for Your Business?

The businesses best placed to succeed in the AI search era are those building a strong, accurate and authoritative presence across multiple channels. Start with these five areas.

1. Clear and Comprehensive Website Content

Your website should answer the questions your ideal customers are asking, in plain language and with genuine expertise. Strong service pages, useful FAQs, detailed guides and clear location information all help search systems understand when your business is relevant. Thin or generic copy gives them very little to work with.

2. Consistent Business Information Everywhere

Your business name, address, phone number, opening hours, services and descriptions should be accurate across your website, Google Business Profile, social channels and directories. Conflicting information creates uncertainty for potential customers and the systems trying to interpret your brand.

3. Regular, Authoritative Content

Blogs, guides, case studies, FAQs and educational resources demonstrate experience and give search platforms more useful context about your expertise. Quality matters more than volume. Publish content that adds something original, answers a real question and is genuinely worth reading.

4. A Strong Third-Party Presence

Reviews, press coverage, industry directories, relevant partnerships and mentions from established organisations can strengthen the wider picture of your business online. They provide independent signals that your brand exists, is active and is trusted by real people.

5. A Well-Optimised Google Business Profile

For a local business, your Google Business Profile remains essential. Keep your categories, services, hours, photos and contact details current, and continue earning genuine customer reviews. AI search has changed discovery, but the fundamentals of local visibility have not disappeared.

The Opportunity Right Now

GEO is still developing, which gives businesses that improve their digital foundations now a valuable head start. Clearer content, better structured information and stronger authority do not only support AI visibility; they also improve traditional search performance and help customers trust your business when they arrive.

The goal is not to chase every new platform or create content for robots. It is to make your expertise unmistakable wherever people search. Businesses that begin now can learn what works, strengthen their presence over time and avoid playing catch-up as AI-assisted discovery becomes more established.
