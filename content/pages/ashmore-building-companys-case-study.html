Driving Consistent Lead Generation for Ashmore Building Company

Introduction

Ashmore Building Company is a construction and renovation business specialising in high-quality residential building projects across the South West. MM Digital partnered with Ashmore to strengthen their online presence and create a reliable system for generating new project enquiries.

Client Overview

Ashmore Building Company focuses on extensions, loft conversions, and high-end residential renovations. Their reputation for craftsmanship and reliability meant that increasing digital visibility could unlock significant opportunities for new projects.

Challenges and Objectives

The primary challenge was transforming Ashmore’s digital presence into a consistent source of new leads. Key challenges included:

• Limited online visibility for local construction searches • A website that required stronger conversion focus • A website that required stronger conversion focus • Competing within a crowded local construction market

The goal was to build a digital system capable of delivering regular and qualified enquiries.

MM Digital’s Approach

MM Digital implemented a strategy designed to attract and convert potential clients.

Website Development and Optimisation: Built and optimised a professional website focused on showcasing projects and converting visitors into enquiries.
Lead Generation Campaigns: Implemented targeted campaigns designed to reach homeowners planning renovations.
Search Visibility Improvements: Optimised digital content to improve local visibility within the South West.
Content Strategy: Developed project-focused content highlighting Ashmore’s craftsmanship and completed work.

Results and Impact

The campaign delivered consistent results for the business.

Key results include:

• 1,000+ local leads generated • 56%+ increase in enquiries • Improved visibility across local search and digital channels • A reliable digital pipeline for new project opportunities.

Conclusion

Through targeted digital marketing and website optimisation, MM Digital helped Ashmore Building Company establish a steady flow of enquiries while strengthening their reputation as a trusted construction provider.
